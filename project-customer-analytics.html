Project Overview

Background

This project explores residential broadband customer performance through customer growth, recurring revenue, network quality, complaints, and churn-related indicators.

The objective was to help business stakeholders monitor key performance indicators and identify areas requiring further investigation through an interactive Tableau dashboard.

Project Details

  • Role: Individual project
  • Period: January 2025 – June 2026
  • Tools: SQL, Google BigQuery, Tableau
  • Deliverables: Interactive dashboard and business recommendations

Methodology

  1. Data Preparation

    Prepared and organized customer, billing, usage, network, and complaint data.

  2. Data Cleaning & Validation

    Reviewed data consistency and validated key metrics before visualization.

  3. Exploratory Data Analysis

    Examined customer growth, revenue trends, network exposure, complaints, and churn.

  4. Dashboard Development

    Designed interactive Tableau views to support stakeholder monitoring.

  5. Business Recommendations

    Translated observed patterns into practical areas for further investigation and action.

BUSINESS INSIGHTS

Key Findings & Business Recommendations

Key patterns observed in the January 2025 – June 2026 analysis and the June 2026 dashboard snapshot.

01

Customer Base Growth

Active customers increased from 1,468 in January 2025 to 18,140 in June 2026, while monthly growth moderated as the customer base expanded.

Business implication

Monitor customer acquisition alongside service quality and retention indicators.

02

Revenue Growth with Stable ARPU

Monthly recurring revenue rose from IDR 0.38 billion to IDR 4.73 billion, while ARPU remained around IDR 260K–261K.

Business implication

Track customer growth and revenue performance together to understand business expansion.

03

Network Exposure by District

In June 2026, overall network exposure was 22.8%. Kepulauan Seribu recorded 92.0%, followed by Cilincing at 86.1% and Ciracas at 85.2%.

Business implication

Prioritize district-level investigation using exposure, customer volume, downtime, latency, and complaint patterns.

04

Network-Related Complaints

In June 2026, the leading complaint categories were intermittent connection (578), slow internet (573), and service down (334).

Business implication

Investigate connection reliability, internet speed, and service availability to improve customer experience.

RECOMMENDED NEXT STEPS

Retention and Revenue Growth Opportunities

  • Test targeted upgrade campaigns for eligible customers considering packages of 100 Mbps and above.
  • Evaluate loyalty benefits or limited-time retention offers for customers showing potential churn risk.
  • Consider payment reminders and more convenient payment options for customers with late-payment patterns.
  • Protect network reliability and service quality while promoting higher-tier packages.

These are proposed actions based on observed patterns. Their effectiveness should be evaluated before drawing conclusions about business impact.
